# Contributing

Moodiary is licensed under [AGPL-3.0](LICENSE). See the [contributor docs](https://docs.moodiary.net/dev/) for setup and workflow.

Before your first pull request is merged, sign the [CLA](CLA.md) through the link the bot posts on it. You keep your copyright, and the project can ship your work, including on the App Store.

Do not add GPL, AGPL, LGPL or unlicensed code or dependencies. They cannot ship on the App Store. Name the source and license of any third-party material in your pull request.
