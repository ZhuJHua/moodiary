Moodiary is an open source diary app that works offline.

Capture
· Rich text: headings, highlights, photos, audio and video
· Timeline and calendar, with category filters
· Mood insights: note how you feel and look back on your year

AI assistant
· Connect your own model provider and keep your own keys
· Ask your diary questions, with tool calling and long-term memory
· Local semantic search that works offline once the model is downloaded

Sync and export
· WebDAV, S3 and local network transfer, with end-to-end encryption
· Export to Markdown, Word, PDF or images
